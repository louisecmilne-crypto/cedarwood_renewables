System Care Packages

Simple care options for existing solar homeowners. Choose the level of ongoing maintenance and support that suits your property and system.

Bronze

Essential Care
  • System condition check
  • Performance readings
  • Visual inspection
  • Maintenance recommendations

Silver

Complete Care
  • Everything included in Bronze
  • Full system check and readings
  • Solar panel cleaning
  • Gutter cleaning
  • Property-wide safety inspection

Gold

Complete Protection
  • Everything included in Silver
  • 4–6 visits/checks per year
  • Full system performance monitoring
  • Panel cleaning and ceramic coating
  • Ceramic coating twice yearly
  • Full property safety inspection
  • Ongoing maintenance recommendations

Package pricing can be tailored to the property and system size, keeping the service straightforward while allowing for different system requirements.
